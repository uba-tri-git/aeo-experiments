The finance rule the generic maps miss

First-party content tends to perform strongest on branded, factual, and entity questions. Discovery and comparison questions tend to rely more on third-party validators. In regulated finance (a "Your Money or Your Life" category) AI favors credentialed, corroborated sources, so you have to win both layers, not just your website.

Source: Contently and Radarly citation data for live financial clients, plus 2026 industry studies (BrightEdge, SE Ranking, Similarweb, 5W, Goodie).

The Destinations

where the answers appear, and how each one sources finance
Tier 1 mass consumer answer reach  ·  Tier 2 material and context-specific, including enterprise  ·  Tier 3 real-time sentiment signal, not a peer answer destination
ChatGPT TIER 1
Largest AI assistant. About 70% of its finance citations are data and news sources (BrightEdge).
AI Overviews TIER 1
Google's in-search answers. ~96% consumer-finance education; video in ~34% (BrightEdge).
Gemini TIER 1
Google index. Rewards clear structure, schema, and answer-first content.
Perplexity TIER 2
Citation-first. Over-indexes Reddit; LinkedIn and G2 feature in B2B answers.
Copilot TIER 2
Bing web plus your internal documents inside Microsoft 365.
Claude TIER 2
Reasoning-heavy with real institutional adoption inside banks and asset managers (Claude Enterprise). Strong on complex, institutional queries.
Grok TIER 3
Real-time X conversations. A market-sentiment signal, not a primary answer destination.
